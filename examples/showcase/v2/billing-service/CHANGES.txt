billing-service v2 adds partial refunds, courier tips and a new cancellation fee. POST /api/v1/refunds accepts an optional `amount` that must not exceed the captured amount minus everything already refunded (otherwise 422 with `remaining_refundable`), and several refunds per order are now allowed (one in flight at a time; new payment status `partially_refunded`, new columns `tip_cents` and `refunded_amount_cents`). The optional `tip` from order.created (v2 payload with `delivery_price` and `tip`) is captured in the same Paylane intent as the delivery price, paid out in full to the courier who delivers, refunded on cancellation before pickup and never refunded after pickup; payment.captured and refund.issued move to schema_version 2 with `tip`, `tip_refunded`, `partial` and `remaining_refundable`. A cancellation after courier assignment now withholds 3.50 EUR instead of 3.00 EUR (free within 2 minutes after the first assignment, as decided by orders-service). Everything else is unchanged from v1.
