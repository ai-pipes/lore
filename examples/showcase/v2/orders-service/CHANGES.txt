orders-service v2 changes the order economics in three places. Cancellation is now free until a courier is assigned and also within 2 minutes after the (first) assignment; after that window the cancellation fee rises from 3.00 EUR to 3.50 EUR (orders/cancel-order, orders/get-order, orders/overview, events/order-cancelled, schemas/orders, ops/stuck-orders). POST /api/v1/orders accepts a new optional `tip` field (0.00-20.00 EUR, max 2 decimals) that is added to the order total, stored in the new `delivery_price` and `tip` columns, carried in the order.created event so billing-service can book it as a courier payout, and refunded in full on cancellation; a tip out of range is rejected with 422 tip_out_of_range (orders/create-order, events/order-created, schemas/orders). Unpaid orders now expire after 10 minutes instead of 15 (jobs/expire-unpaid-orders, orders/create-order, orders/overview, events/order-created, events/order-cancelled, schemas/orders, ops/stuck-orders).
