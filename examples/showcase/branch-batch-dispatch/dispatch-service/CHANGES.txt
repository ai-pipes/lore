Feature branch batch-dispatch for dispatch-service introduces batched dispatch: before searching for a courier, dispatch/assign-courier now asks the new dispatch/batch-matcher service to pair the order with another searching order whose pickup is within 600 m and whose direction is compatible (bearings within 45 degrees, at most +25% detour per order, combined weight up to 20 kg), so up to 2 orders can be offered to one courier as a single batch. Batch offers time out after 60 s instead of 45 s, are accepted or declined as a whole, are split back into single orders after two declines, and yield one courier.assigned per order; the assignments table gains `batch_id` and `batch_seq`. dispatch/overview describes the new flow; all other documents are unchanged from v1.
